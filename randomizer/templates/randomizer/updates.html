Version History

9.0 Updates

Version 9.0.0

General Changes
  • Assembly of world areas, character models, event scripts, and sprite assets has been completely refactored. As such, minor bug fixes pertaining to those things in 8.2.x will not be enumerated here.
Changes related to characters, equipment, and party spells
  • Starting party size can be any number from 1 to 5.
  • A cap on the total number of recruitable characters has been added, which does not necessarily need to be determined by excluding specific characters.
    • This is separate from character-specific exclusions.
    • i.e. If you want only 2 characters to be available in the seed, but also deliberately exclude Bowser and Mallow, your 2 available characters would therefore be any two of: Mario, Toadstool, Geno.
  • Equipment assignment to characters has been expanded to allow several new options (i.e. "random, except anyone can wear any accessory").
  • "Some buffs added" is now an option for equipment stats, which adds some extra non-random properties to vanilla equipment and converts some weapons to increase your magic attack instead of physical attack.
  • Added option to make the Signal Ring alert you of unclaimed star pieces when entering a world area.
  • All stat randomization algorithms have been rebalanced.
  • Added option to randomize party spell elements.
    • When enabled, spell names are changed to reflect their elements.
    • At the moment, only spells which were originally infused with an element (Jump, Snowy, etc) can have a randomized element.
  • Added option to uncap Super Jumps.
  • The shortened frame window on Group Hug is (tentatively) fixed.
  • Added option to pick and choose which party spells can and cannot be learnable in the seed.
    • Excluding Super Jump will make some checks inaccessible, but this will never make a seed incompletable.
  • If EXP from regular encounters is turned off, the player is guaranteed to start off with at least one damage-inducing non-elemental spell to ensure that transforming Mokura does not softlock your progress.
Changes related to check locations and item placement
  • Added option to cap the number of Star Pieces available in the world.
  • Mokura's cloud, Chester's battle door, Dojo 2, Dojo 3, and all Factory bosses (including Smithy's lair) are eligible to grant Star Pieces.
    • See the Progression Logic updates section for clarification on Smithy's lair.
  • Any boss location, not just Culex's door, can be excluded from eligibility to hold a Star Piece.
  • Added option to ensure that only up to 1 Star Piece will be available per each of the seven World Map mega-sections (depending on other settings).
  • The option to shuffle original item pools by world area has been changed to shuffle the entire game's item pool within itself.
  • The pool of eligible chests to contain EXP stars has been expanded.
  • Added option for mimic chests to appear anywhere.
    • If you scale bosses by world area, this setting respects that choice and will not put a lategame-strength mimic in an earlygame area.
  • Added option for slot roulette chests to appear (almost) anywhere.
  • Added option for Beetlemania to be shuffled into the item pool as a key item. With this setting, the Game Boy Kid sells you a Key Item check.
  • Toad is always available in the Rose Town Inn to give you an item on your first stay regardless of the town's liberation status.
  • Added option for the infinite coin chest (normally in Magikoopa's room) to be shuffled into one random chest.
  • Added option to shuffle the four Marrymore wedding gear pieces randomly throughout the world.
    • All four must be found to fight the Marrymore boss.
    • They are NOT considered Key Items as far as shuffle logic is concerned, but they appear in your inventory as Special Items and cannot be sold.
    • The snifits, plus the item on the tower boss' head near the altar, become item pool checks.
  • Added choice to have empty chests perform "You Missed!", or simply be disabled from the outset.
  • Added option to move the three invisible items (normally in Mario's Pad, Rose Town, and Yo'ster Isle). The Three Musty Fears will give clues as to their new locations. The items you find will not necessarily be flags.
    • These cannot be excluded from eligibility to hold a progress item.
  • The original behaviour of the Gardener has been restored, and players must complete both Forest Maze and Marrymore to access him.
  • Both items in the first Nimbus Castle cellar are always available regardless of the town's liberation status.
  • Added option to expand eligible Star Piece locations to include boss fights as well as general item pool checks.
  • Any item check, not just 3D maze/Culex's lair/30 SJs/100 SJs, can be excluded from eligibility to contain a progression item.
    • If "Special Items can appear in the general item pool" is turned off, any choices to exclude Key Item locations will be ignored.
    • The item check pool has been vastly expanded.
      • Every treasure chest (except the Grate Guy's Casino slot machine chest) is included.
      • Every sidequest reward which can originally only be obtained once is included. (i.e. Marrymore Suite gifts are included, but bellhop tips are not)
      • Every freestanding item (except for gold coins and Midas waterfall climb tiny frog coins) is included.
      • Freestanding items which are excluded from eligibility will always be defaulted to their original vanilla item. Chests and sidequests rewards that are excluded will still be randomized, but will not contain progression items (key item, progressive fireworks, star pieces, or Marrymore gear).
  • Checks which require a lot of repetition (Knife Guy's juggling game, Look The Other Way, Marrymore suite gifts) can have their thresholds adjusted as the player sees fit.
    • Super Jump checks are usually biased to hold better equipment, but this does not apply if the thresholds are lowered.
  • To prevent players from believing they are softlocked when they have missed checks, Frogfucius will hint you toward one check you have not done yet. Be warned that this hint system does not account for checks that the player has excluded.
Changes to item behaviour and availability
  • "Proxy items" (superfluous single items that are simply exchanged for one other item with no co-requirements) have been changed such that they require at least one other condition in order to be exchanged.
    • The Wallet can only be exchanged after completing Bandit's Way.
    • The RareFrogCoin can only be exchanged after liberating the Mushroom Kingdom.
    • The Cricket Jam can only be exchanged after the Cricket Pie.
  • Added new options for Fireworks.
    • Vanilla: Like in the original game, Players can purchase up to one Fireworks at a time from the Moleville house, exchange it outside for a Shiny Stone, and exchange it in the shop for a Carbo Cookie.
    • Shuffle Fireworks:
      • One Fireworks is shuffled somewhere in the game (treated as a Key Item).
      • The Fireworks house sells you a random item check (this is a Key Item check).
      • The Fireworks can be traded for a Shiny Stone outdoors as normal, and the Shiny Stone can be traded for the Carbo Cookie in the shop as normal.
      • You may ask the shop girl for your Shiny Stone back if you need it for Culex's door, after you have traded away the Carbo Cookie.
    • Progressive Fireworks:
      • Fireworks, Shiny Stone, and Carbo Cookie are each shuffled somewhere in the world. These are NOT treated as Key Items as far as the shuffler is concerned, but they will appear in your Special Items inventory. The three items will always be found in that order.
      • The Fireworks house sells you a random item pool check (this is NOT a Key Item check).
      • The Pur-Tend Store and item shop girls will not trade with you.
      • Culex's door will automatically open as soon as you find the Shiny Stone.
    • With the latter two options, you may trade the Carbo Cookie for a general pool item check if the Factory bucket warp is turned off.
    • With the latter two options, your ending credits fireworks will be random.
    • With the latter two options, the Fireworks, Shiny Stone, and Carbo Cookie cannot be sold.
  • The Seed, Fertilizer, and Bright Card are always key items.
    • The Bright Card can still be sold and re-bought from the gambler in Marrymore.
  • Reusable items (Star Egg, See Ya, Sheep Attack, etc) can only be obtained once per seed.
    • Exception: The Mystery Egg, Lamb's Lure, and Sheep Attack each will progressively upgrade whichever version of the item you currently have (exactly 3 instances total are available in the seed). If you upgraded any of these manually, and you already have the Sheep Attack by the time you find the third one, you will instead be given a random top-tier consumable.
  • Added option to exclude the Star Egg from appearing anywhere.
  • Added option to restore the original game's multi-hit coin chest behaviour. Also applies to chests containing multiple Frog Coins.
  • Added new options for scaling EXP stars that are based on how many boss fights you have completed.
Changes to progression logic
  • To introduce more interesting logic chains, many areas have options to gate them behind completing a certain task (recruit a specific character, defeat a specific boss, clear a specific area, etc...)
    • These are approximately based around events that happened in the original game at the times when these areas became available.
    • The areas that can be gated this way are: Bandit's Way, Forest Maze, Moleville Mines, Pipe Vault, Booster Tower, Marrymore chapel, Sea & Sunken Ship, Belome Temple, Monstro Town, Barrel Volcano, Bowser's Keep, and Factory.
    • The Seaside Town boss fight can also be gated in this manner, which will leave the area boss and their minions in the surrounding houses until the condition is completed.
    • Some of these gates remove areas from the world map, while others prohibit access by means of in-area obstacles.
    • You will always be able to complete a circle around the overworld map. When Bowser's Keep is closed, a path from Vista Hill to Nimbus Land bypasses it until it opens.
    • Depending on how close the task is to the area it unlocks, you may be shown a message confirming that you unlocked an area.
  • The Factory is no longer necessarily gated behind having found all your Star Pieces.
    • This means the Factory may have Star Pieces and Key Items depending on your settings.
  • Added option that despawns the three invisible check items until you have visited the 3 Musty Fears inn.
  • Added option that converts the Midas River warp in the Carbo Cookie girl's bucket to warp you to the final Factory boss if you have enough Star Pieces.
    • If disabled, the girl gives you a random item check when you trade the Carbo Cookie under settings where the Carbo Cookie is a key item.
  • Grate Guy's Casino warp has been converted to a trampoline inside of the casino, instead of talking to Grate Guy.
  • Expanded option to adjust how many Star Pieces you need to access the final Factory boss. Instead of 6 or 7, it can now be 0 to 7.
    • This can be less than the total number of Star Pieces available in the game.
    • The green button to take you to Smithy's lair in the Factory will not be usable until you have this many Star Pieces.
    • If the Factory Warp is enabled, the trampoline will not appear until you have this many Star Pieces.
    • If the Bucket Warp is enabled, the bucket will warp you to Midas River instead until you have this many Star Pieces.
  • There are now four different options for a condition that terminates the game.
    • Beat the final Factory Boss: This was the original condition to beat the game. Under this setting, the final Factory Boss cannot be assigned a Star Piece, although the final Star Piece cutscene will play when you win.
    • Beat Smithy: Once you find Smithy anywhere in the world and beat him, the game is over.
    • Collect required Star Pieces: Once you collect the Star Pieces necessary to access the Factory boss, the game is over, without having to do that fight.
    • Beat Monstro Town sealed door: The game is over when you unlock the door to Culex's lair and defeat whoever is behind it. Under this setting, Culex's lair cannot be assigned a Star Piece, although the final Star Piece cutscene will play when you win.
Changes to shops
  • The Room Service menu in the Marrymore suite now sells two consumables and is considered part of the shop pool.
  • The Moleville swap shop will sell 3 random non-reusable damage items.
  • The Frog Disciple always sells exactly five items, typically those that are subject to limited quantity, and only items that can be sold back to shops (no key items).
  • The Moleville treasure shop toad may sell key items or Star Pieces (if your settings allow for this), or items that were only available in limited quantities in the original game.
  • Some items can no longer be purchased in regular shops. This includes:
    • Reusable items (Star Egg, See Ya, Lamb's Lure, etc)
    • Fireworks, Shiny Stone, and Carbo Cookie
    • Mystery Egg, Lamb's Lure, and Sheep Attack
  • Each item in the game is no longer guaranteed to be in at least one shop.
  • Added an option to exclude Pick-Me-Ups from shops.
  • To prevent rare cases of softlocking in the event of physical attack immunities and spell exclusions, at least one shop available from the beginning of the seed will carry Bad Mushrooms.
  • Item prices are no longer randomized, but equipment prices are still based on their stat distribution.
  • Empty shops will simply display a dialog instead of just containing a Goodie Bag.
Changes specific to world areas
  • Added an option that does the following:
    • For items that are repeatably grantable (such as Marrymore bellhop tips), the incidence of the prizes is improved and the quality of prizes is expanded.
    • Mushroom Boy's prize grant odds are improved.
    • The Mushroom Derby's odds for Yoshi are improved.
    • Forest Maze mushrooms may be any kind of mushroom.
    • The spawn rate for Mokura's cloud is improved.
  • The hallway chest in Mushroom Kingdom has been moved back above the door.
  • Added an option to randomize the three Melody Bay songs. Hints are given by Frogfucius' scroll, the mining moles, the Monstro Town starslap, and the protégé tadpole.
  • The second hidden NPC in Rose Town behind the lower house remains available even after defeating Bowyer.
  • If you fail three times in a row to obtain enough points in Goomba Thumpin' for the second prize, the gamekeeper will reward you the prize out of sympathy.
  • Added an option to skip the mandatory Moleville Mountain minigame. It can still be played optionally by returning to Moleville after completing the Mines.
  • The blue toad in Moleville will describe the items he is selling to you. Some items may have the same description. Depending on your settings, he may have Key Items or Star Pieces.
  • You can now save your game while working as a bellhop in Marrymore.
  • Added an option to randomize the Sunken Ship password. At minimum, 6 hints are available within the ship. The shop shaman will still tell you how close you are to being correct.
  • Johnny's periscope will show you if the green button in the Factory is ready yet or not, and will tell you how many Star Pieces are needed for it.
  • The Shy Away in the Bean Valley five-pipe always flies in a circle counter-clockwise, starting with the most rightmost pipe.
  • Players can press A, B, X, or Y during Dr. Topper's barrel counting game to abort the timer and go straight to the answer input.
  • Players can forfeit the green button puzzle room in Bowser's Keep by attempting to leave the room via its entrance.
  • There is now a trampoline in the save room before Magikoopa's room that will eject you from Bowser's Keep upon confirmation. This means you will no longer warp out of the castle upon a Game Over.
  • Players can no longer save between the final two bosses of Bowser's Keep. They are back-to-back like in the original game.
  • Added an option that enables both of the following:
    • Climbing to the top of Booster's Tower, if you have already cleared the balcony fight, will warp you back to the ground.
    • Reaching the Inner Factory will add a dot for it to the overworld map, and will place an exit trampoline in the first room.
Changes to enemies
  • Added a third boss scaling option, which performs the area-based scaling and then performs an ADDITIONAL shuffle on the boss placements.
  • Boss substitution has been improved. For example, Dodo's statue minigame will show the actual boss of that location attacking the statues, and will also be seen slowly escaping the final castle hall.
  • More bosses have henchmen that will replace sprites where appropriate (i.e. the Snifits in Booster Tower.)
  • More boss locations have henchmen that can be replaced (i.e. the birds at the end of Nimbus Castle).
  • Some boss and henchmen replacements can be disabled visually to preserve visual cues for minigames or strategy for event battles.
  • Players can choose which bosses they would and would not like to include in the location shuffler (instead of just excluding Culex).
    • i.e. Smithy can be shuffled into the pool, but will stay in the final Factory position if he is not selected in this setting.
  • Replaced henchmen that start a battle will now have you actually fight the henchman you see.
    • i.e. If Culex owns Booster Tower, three of the Crystals replace the Snifits, and you may have to fight a solo Crystal if you talk to one.
  • Enemy immunity shuffling has been expanded to include weapon damage as an immunity.
  • The following actions can now grant you a Star Piece, if the skipped boss was supposed to grant one:
    • Completing the Booster Tower curtain minigame
    • Completing the statue polishing minigame
    • Failing a slots roulette chest and defeating the forced mimic fight
    • There is also an option that blocks you from obtaining Star Pieces in these ways (as well as Mack Skip), and forces you to fight the boss regardless of whether you succeeded at these tasks or not.
  • Enemies which may need to appear in more than one location (i.e. Punchinello's bob-ombs vs. bob-ombs in Moleville Mines) have been separated so that only those related to a shuffled boss will have their stats changed.
    • When scaled, this also means Bahamutt will have different stats when summoned by Chester and Magikoopa.
    • This also restores the original Mad Mallet fight in the Factory (when not substituted by henchmen).
  • The unused Aero enemy has been given a single attack animation, a recoil animation, switched upright, and is used as a henchman battle where appropriate for Bowyer.
  • In the Valentina fight, Dodo will 1v1 one of your characters if you have a party size of 2 or more. If you only have one character, Dodo will join Valentina to fight alongside her immediately.
  • Birdetta will no longer hatch out of an eggshell unless she is in her vanilla location. The boss fight at the giant egg location in Nimbus Land will hatch out of the eggshell.
Cosmetic & Text Changes
  • A new title screen has been added.
  • The intro movie has been removed in order to free up assets.
  • Added option to play in the overworld as whichever character is shown on the file select menu.
  • Cosmetic settings are no longer tied to the seed value.
  • Character name changes are now a separate option from palette changes.
  • Some additional character palettes have been added.
  • Changed character names now appear correctly in the level-up menu.
  • Spell names which include a character's name will be changed to use the name of the character who learns it.
    • This only happens if character names are not changed.
  • Walking animations have been restored in battles (i.e. Cloaker/Domino, King Calamari).
  • Most abrupt endings/transitions in boss fights have been smoothed out.
  • More detail has been given to make freestanding items and items popping out of treasure chests more closely match their contents in appearance.
    • Some sprites are used for many items, so players will still have to pick them up to find out what they are.
  • All boss sprites now resemble the actual boss.
  • All three stages of the second Dojo occupant are now visible. Your opponent will transform to its next form in the dojo after you win each stage.
  • Added option to differentiate shared-sprite bosses (Croco 1&2, Belome 1&2, Jinx 1-3, and all 4 mimic enemies) in the overworld.
  • All Nimbus Land statues are gold-coloured.
  • Any overworld locations which CAN have a character, but where you will NOT be recruiting a character, will show Toad.
  • NPC dialog has been vastly expanded, and some of it even depends on which boss is occupying an area. Many NPCs will offer you hints regarding easy-to-miss checks, the locations of bosses, and other settings enabled for your seed.
  • In Rose Town, the boy who was frozen as a statue has been moved safely inside his house, and the dad from the treasure house has been moved indoors with the stairs permanently set.
  • Samus stays in the Mushroom Kingdom permanently after liberating it.
  • Psychopath messages have been slightly reformatted for clarity.
  • The Beetlemania menu tutorial has been removed.
  • Added an option to use ABXY button colours from the Japanese version.
  • Added a DMCA-friendly option to remove specific tracks from the random boss music pool.
  • Added an option to remove some flashing animations from the game. This is not a perfect implementation and we cannot promise that the randomizer is 100% safe for photosensitive players or viewers..
  • Some palette glitches have been fixed.
Other Site Updates
  • Settings interfaced has been revamped.
  • New presets added.
  • Player's guide revamped for new changes.
  • Fleshed out a credits page.
